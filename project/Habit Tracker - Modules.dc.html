BRIEFAssumptions & reasoning
Model

Modules describe themselves

Each module declares its inputs, unit, goals and milestones, plus one to three dashboard widgets. The Dashboard is a widget host, not a hand-built page — activating Reading adds its widgets in place. Same widget definitions render on both platforms, so parity is structural rather than maintained by hand.

Crosstalk

Read-only published series

A module publishes named series (Weight → weight.daily, Calories → calories.daily). Any widget can subscribe. The Weight × Calories chart is a widget that owns neither series, which is the pattern to prove out first.

Spine

The calendar is the join key

Every entry is timestamped, so every module can render a one-line day summary. The Log is module-agnostic: text in, AI parse, typed draft rows, approve, routed to the owning module. Nothing writes to a module without passing review.

Taken from your brief

Weeks of existing data. Plain, neutral copy. Same features on both platforms, laid out natively per platform. Reading tracks pages against books you add (title, author, cover) with an optional note per log. Dashboard layout variations: 1a and 1b; module-count variations in 2a and 2b, which follow the 1a grid.

Open questions

The repository is empty, so nothing here is recreated from source. Four calls I made and would flag: goals are recurring while milestones are one-off (both shown on the calendar); widgets are fixed per module for now, not user-arranged; an unmatched food becomes a draft row you can price by hand; sharing is per-module, not all-or-nothing. Saved meals hold either a combination or a single item, and are managed inside the Calories module.
